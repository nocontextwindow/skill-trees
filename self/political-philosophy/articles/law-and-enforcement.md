# Law & Enforcement

`POL_LAW` · [← Political Philosophy](../index.md)

Whether law is grounded in something beyond enactment, and what punishment is for.

## Questions

- Can an unjust law be law? What turns on the answer?
- What is punishment for, and does my answer survive hard cases?
- Is rule of law a real constraint on power or a legitimating story?
- How much discretion should enforcers have, given that zero is impossible?
- What makes law binding on those who had no part in making it?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Natural law: an unjust law is no law at all.
- Legal positivism: law is whatever the recognized process produces; its merit is a separate question.
- Punishment theories: retribution, deterrence, incapacitation, rehabilitation, restoration.
- Fuller's internal morality of law — generality, publicity, non-retroactivity — as a middle position.
- Selective enforcement as the practical hole in rule-of-law claims.
