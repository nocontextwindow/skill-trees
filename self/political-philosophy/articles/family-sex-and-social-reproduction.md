# Family, Sex & Social Reproduction

`POL_FAMILY` · [← Political Philosophy](../index.md)

How a society produces its next generation and transmits itself — a political question, not merely a private one.

## Questions

- What's the strongest form of the feminist case, and precisely where does my disagreement begin — on facts, values, or strategy?
- Is there a secular case for restrictive sexual norms that stands without religious premises, and does it hold?
- What's actually wrong with hedonism as an organizing principle, in argument rather than distaste?
- Is the family a pre-political institution the state should defer to, or one arrangement among several?
- How much of what looks like nature here is convention, and how would I tell?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Disputes here routinely conflate three separable claims: what follows empirically, what should be valued, and whether a proposed remedy works.
- "The personal is political" versus a protected private sphere.
- Evolutionary accounts of pair-bonding vs. social-construction accounts — and the is/ought gap between either and any norm.
- Fertility decline as the empirical pressure point everyone cites and few explain.
- Liberal reply: arrangements should be chosen, and the burden is on whoever would restrict.
