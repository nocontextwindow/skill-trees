# Empire, Conquest & Historical Power

`POL_EMPIRE` · [← Political Philosophy](../index.md)

How political orders expand and dominate others, and what follows morally and materially.

## Questions

- How much of the modern West's wealth and institutions is attributable to imperialism? What evidence would settle it?
- What's the strongest post-colonial case, at full strength, before any counter?
- Does the historical answer change my other political commitments, or are they independent?
- Is there a coherent principle for what present people owe for past conquest?
- What distinguishes empire from other forms of large-scale political integration?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Extraction thesis: development was financed by conquest and plunder.
- Institutional thesis: growth came mainly from internal institutional change, with colonies a poor investment.
- Said on how empire shaped the categories through which it was described.
- Counterfactual difficulty: no clean comparison case exists.
- The empirical question and the reparative question are separable and routinely run together.
