# Ideology, Persuasion & Manufactured Belief

`POL_IDEOLOGY` · [← Political Philosophy](../index.md)

How political belief is produced at scale — framing, repetition, and control over which questions are askable.

## Questions

- Is there a principled line between persuasion and manipulation — consent, transparency, reversibility — or only degree?
- Who controls which questions are askable, by what mechanism rather than conspiracy?
- Can a population be said to hold a belief freely if the alternatives were never presented?
- How much of my own political belief would survive learning how it was formed?
- Is ideology an error to be corrected or an unavoidable feature of political cognition?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Manufacturing consent: structural filters, not coordination, produce the effect.
- Overton window management as the real lever, upstream of any particular argument.
- Marxist false consciousness — and the standard objection that it's unfalsifiable and condescending.
- Propaganda-model critics point out audiences resist far more than the model predicts.
- The hard part is a distinction that doesn't reduce to "framing I agree with."
