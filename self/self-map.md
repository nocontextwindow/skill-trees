# Self-Map

My own mapping: personality, proclivities, what reliably moves me and what
reliably stalls me. Who I am when nobody's watching — the internal
counterpart to [Social Identity](social-identity.md).

This is a working document, not a personality-test result. The value is in
the parts that are specific and slightly unflattering; the parts that
would fit anyone are noise.

## Open questions

- How much of what I call personality is fixed temperament versus habit I
  could change if I decided to?
- Which of my failure modes are worth fixing, and which are the cost of
  something I'd rather keep?
- Am I optimizing for the life I want or the one that's legible to people
  I want to impress?
- What would I have to admit is true about me for this document to be
  actually useful rather than flattering?
- Where do I get the most reliable outside signal on myself, and do I
  actually listen to it?
- What's changed about me in the last five years, and was any of it
  deliberate?
