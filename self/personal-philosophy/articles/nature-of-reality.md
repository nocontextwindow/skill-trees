# Nature of Reality

`PP_REALITY` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

Whether there is a reality independent of observers, and whether it's knowable.

## Questions

- Is reality independent of consciousness — and how would I know without begging the question?
- Does a mind-independent world guarantee anything about our access to it?
- Where do abstractions live — numbers, universals, categories?
- Does anything in how I live depend on the answer?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Realism: things are what they are regardless of belief.
- Idealism / constructivism: what we call reality is partly constituted by mind, language, or social agreement.
- Kant's split: there's a real world, but we only ever access it through our own categories.
- Pragmatist dodge: the question is idle because both answers predict the same experience.
