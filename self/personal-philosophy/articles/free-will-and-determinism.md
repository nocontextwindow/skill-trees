# Free Will & Determinism

`PP_FREEWILL` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

Whether choice is real if every event is fixed by prior causes.

## Questions

- Is volition a genuine causal power, or the experience of a process already determined?
- Can moral responsibility survive hard determinism? If not, what replaces it?
- If choice is real, at what level — over actions, over attention, over what I let myself think about?
- Does my answer match how I actually treat other people's failures?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Hard determinism: no free will, and the concept should be retired.
- Libertarian free will: some events are genuinely up to the agent.
- Compatibilism: freedom is acting on your own reasons without external compulsion.
- Objection to compatibilism: it renames the problem rather than solving it.
- Randomness doesn't help — an uncaused choice isn't more yours than a caused one.
