# Sense of Life

`PP_SENSE` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

The pre-conceptual, emotionally integrated appraisal of existence that operates beneath explicit belief.

## Questions

- What is mine, stated plainly — and does it match the philosophy I claim?
- Where did it come from, and how much is revisable?
- Can it be deliberately changed, or only shifted by what I expose myself to?
- Is a benevolent-universe premise a factual claim, a psychological stance, or a choice?
- What does my involuntary reaction to others' success or failure reveal about it?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Rand's framing: a pre-conceptual equivalent of metaphysics — an implicit view of existence.
- It can conflict with stated philosophy, and usually wins when it does.
- Deflationary reading: this is just temperament and mood, dressed up as metaphysics.
- Adjacent concepts: Heidegger's attunement, dispositional optimism, the "basic trust" of attachment theory.
