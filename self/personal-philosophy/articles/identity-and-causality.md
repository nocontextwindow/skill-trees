# Identity & Causality

`PP_IDENTITY` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

That a thing is what it is, and acts according to what it is.

## Questions

- Is causality a feature of reality or of how we model it?
- Does the law of identity do real work, or is it an empty tautology?
- What survives of identity when a thing changes over time — including me?
- Can something be genuinely uncaused, and would that break anything I need?
- How much does a macroscopic account of causality owe to physics?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Hume: we observe constant conjunction and infer a necessity we never perceive.
- Causality as identity applied to action, not a separate brute fact.
- Regularity vs. counterfactual vs. interventionist accounts of cause.
- Quantum indeterminacy as a challenge to universal causation — or as irrelevant at human scale.
