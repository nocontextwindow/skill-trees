# What a Philosophy Is For

`PP_WHATFOR` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

What job the system is supposed to do — describe reality, guide action, hold a group together, or make life bearable.

## Questions

- What is mine actually for, and am I honest about which job I want it to do?
- If a philosophy were true but corrosive to live by, would I want it?
- Is it possible to not run on a philosophy, or only to run on an unexamined one?
- How much of what I believe was chosen versus absorbed?
- Does it have to be livable by others, or only by me?

## Challenges & perspectives

- These jobs come apart — one optimized for consolation isn't one optimized for accuracy.
- Pragmatism: truth just is what works, so the tension dissolves.
- Therapeutic view: philosophy's job is dissolving confusion, not building systems.
- Objection: "everyone has a philosophy" overstates it — most people have habits.
