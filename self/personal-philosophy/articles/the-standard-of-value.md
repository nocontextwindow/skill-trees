# The Standard of Value

`PP_STANDARD` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

The measure against which everything else counts as good or bad.

## Questions

- What is my standard, in one sentence, and does it decide hard cases or just sound good?
- Can an "ought" be derived from an "is"? If not, where does normativity enter?
- If life is the standard: whose, over what horizon, and how are conflicts between lives resolved?
- Is happiness the goal, the byproduct, or a bad target that corrupts when aimed at?
- Does my stated standard match the one revealed by my choices?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Candidates: life/flourishing, pleasure, preference satisfaction, duty, divine command, social utility.
- Neo-Aristotelian move: derive the ought from facts about what kind of entity we are.
- Hume's guillotine: no ought from is.
- Objection: life-as-standard either smuggles in normativity or is too thin to settle cases.
- Divine command: answers the grounding question but relocates it (Euthyphro).
