## Personality Models (`PSY_PERSONALITY`)

Big Five (OCEAN) as the empirically best-replicated trait model versus
Jungian/MBTI-style typologies (weaker empirical support, but useful as a
qualitative vocabulary for self-reflection). Know your own Big Five profile
well enough to predict your own failure modes under stress.

## Ideal Self Concept (`PSY_IDEAL`)

The gap between actual self and ideal self is a major driver of motivation
and, when too large or too vague, of anxiety and paralysis. Compare to the
Platonic notion of the Form as a perfect template a particular thing only
ever approximates — is the "ideal self" a fixed target or something that
should update as you grow?

## Self-Authoring / Personal Narrative Construction (`PSY_AUTHOR`)

Explicitly writing your own past (making sense of formative events),
present (values, personality), and future (goals, a concrete plan) rather
than leaving your self-narrative implicit and unexamined.
