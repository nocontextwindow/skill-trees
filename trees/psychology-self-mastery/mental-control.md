## Cognitive Control & Meta-Cognition (`PSY_COGCTRL`)

The actual target of "controlling your own mind": noticing your own
thought patterns in real time (metacognition), and having deliberate
techniques (reframing, cognitive defusion, attentional control) to
interrupt unhelpful ones rather than being run by them.
