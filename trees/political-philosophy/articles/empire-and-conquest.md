# Empire, Conquest & Historical Power

[← Political Philosophy](../index.md) · `POL_EMPIRE`

How political orders expand, absorb, and dominate others, and what follows
morally and materially from that history. The empirical question — how
much of present wealth and institutional capacity is attributable to
conquest and extraction — is separable from the normative one about what,
if anything, is owed now, and the two are routinely run together by both
sides.

## Open questions

- How much of the modern West's wealth and institutions is actually
  attributable to imperialism? What evidence would settle it?
- What's the strongest post-colonial case, stated at full strength before
  any counter?
- Does the answer to the historical question change any of my other
  political commitments, or are they independent?
- Is there a coherent principle for what present people owe for past
  conquest?
- What distinguishes empire from other forms of large-scale political
  integration, if anything?
