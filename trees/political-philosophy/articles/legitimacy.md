# What Makes Power Legitimate

`POL_LEGIT` · [← Political Philosophy](../index.md)

The difference between authority and mere force.

## Questions

- What makes power legitimate rather than merely effective?
- Can an illegitimately established order become legitimate over time, and how?
- Is legitimacy a fact about the order or a belief held by the governed?
- Does an order lose legitimacy by acting badly, or only by how it was constituted?
- Who has standing to judge?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Weber's three types: traditional, charismatic, legal-rational.
- Consent, divine sanction, competence, procedure, and results as rival sources.
- Descriptive vs. normative legitimacy — believed-legitimate is not the same as is-legitimate.
- Practical stake: a legitimate order is far cheaper to run than one held by coercion.
- Realist objection: legitimacy is a story power tells after winning.
