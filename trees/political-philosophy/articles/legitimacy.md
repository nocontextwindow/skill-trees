# What Makes Power Legitimate

[← Political Philosophy](../index.md) · `POL_LEGIT`

The difference between authority and mere force. Candidate sources:
consent of the governed, tradition and continuity, divine sanction,
demonstrated competence, procedural correctness, or simply results.
Legitimacy matters practically because it determines whether compliance
requires ongoing coercion — an order widely seen as legitimate is far
cheaper to run than one that isn't.

## Open questions

- What actually makes power legitimate rather than merely effective?
- Can an illegitimately established order become legitimate over time, and
  by what mechanism?
- Is legitimacy a fact about the order or a belief held by the governed —
  and does the distinction survive scrutiny?
- Does a legitimate order lose legitimacy by acting badly, or only by
  changing how it was constituted?
- Who has standing to judge legitimacy?
