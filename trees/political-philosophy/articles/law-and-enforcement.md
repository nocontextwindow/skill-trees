# Law & Enforcement

[← Political Philosophy](../index.md) · `POL_LAW`

Whether law is grounded in something beyond enactment — a natural standard
against which unjust law fails to be law at all — or is simply whatever
the recognized process produces. Downstream: what punishment is for
(retribution, deterrence, incapacitation, restoration), and whether rule
of law is a genuine constraint or a description of how power prefers to
present itself.

## Open questions

- Can an unjust law be law? What turns on the answer?
- What is punishment actually for, and does my answer survive hard cases?
- Is rule of law a real constraint on power or a legitimating story?
- How much discretion should enforcers have, given that zero is impossible?
- What makes law binding on those who had no part in making it?
