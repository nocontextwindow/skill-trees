### Do rights exist prior to government, or only because government grants and enforces them? `POL_ROOT`

Locke: natural rights precede the state, government exists to secure them.
Hobbes: rights are what's left after you exit a state of nature so brutal
that almost any sovereign is an improvement. Rousseau: legitimacy comes
from the general will, not a pre-political rights baseline. What's the
strongest version of the "rights are only social constructs" position, and

### How much of the Western individual-rights tradition is actually downstream of Christianity? `POL_CHRISTIAN`

(*Dominion*): the very idea of the individual as a bearer of rights,
independent of tribe/caste/family role, has specifically Christian
theological roots (equality before God, conscience as an inviolable inner
space). If that's right — does Western liberalism survive Christianity's
