## Modern Institutional Power — Network & Venture Power (`PWR_MODERN`)

The a16z model as a case study: power built not through formal hierarchy
but through network position — being the node that connects capital,
talent, and narrative. Marc Andreessen's "It's Time to Build" essay and the
firm's "American Dynamism" thesis are explicit articulations of using
institutional power to shape which future gets built, not just to fund it.
