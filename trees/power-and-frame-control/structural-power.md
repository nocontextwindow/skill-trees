## Supremacy & Dominance Dynamics (`PWR_DOMINANCE`)

Dominance hierarchies versus prestige hierarchies (status earned through
demonstrated competence and freely given deference, vs. status enforced
through the threat of cost) — most real human hierarchies are a blend of
both, and knowing which one you're actually operating in changes the
correct strategy.
