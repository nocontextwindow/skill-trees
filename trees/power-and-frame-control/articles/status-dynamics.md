# Status Dynamics

[← Power & Frame Control](../index.md) · `PWR_STATUS`

Status is the deference a group actually extends, and it's distinct from
power (control over outcomes and resources) and from authority (formal
position) — people confuse all three constantly and to their cost. The
dominance/prestige distinction is one of the more robust findings here:
deference given out of fear behaves differently from deference freely
granted for demonstrated value, and the two routes predict different
behaviour from the people holding each. Status is also local and largely
zero-sum within a group, which is why so much of what looks like ambition
is really group selection — moving to a room where your currency counts.
The uncomfortable part is that status-seeking is mostly invisible to the
person doing it, and self-reports of not caring about status are close to
worthless as evidence.

## Open questions

- Do dominance and prestige feel different from the inside, or do people
  systematically misreport which route they're on?
- Can you opt out of status competition, or does opting out just enter you
  into a different game with the same rules?
- Is status a proxy for something real — competence, contribution — or a
  self-referential market that has floated free of its referent?
- How much of what you want is wanted, and how much is wanted because it's
  ranked?
- What is the right response to being in a group whose status currency you
  don't respect?
