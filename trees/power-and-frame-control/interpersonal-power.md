## Frame Control Fundamentals (`PWR_FRAME`)

A "frame" is the implicit context that determines how everything said or
done inside it gets interpreted. Whoever's frame prevails in an interaction
sets the rules without having to argue for them explicitly. The core skill:
noticing when you're inside someone else's frame, and how to hold your own
under social pressure without becoming rigid or defensive about it.

## Boundaries as Power Maintenance (`PWR_BOUND`)

A boundary is a frame applied to yourself — a stated (or enforced) limit on
what you'll accept. Weak boundaries are a direct loss of frame control:
without a clear "no," your frame is whatever the other person decides it
is.

## Status Dynamics (`PWR_STATUS`)

Status transactions happen constantly and mostly nonverbally — status is
relative, contextual, and can be raised or lowered by anyone in an
interaction regardless of formal hierarchy. Learn to read status moves
before learning to make them.

## Masculinity / Femininity Polarity Dynamics (`PWR_POLARITY`)

The claim (from polarity-dynamics frameworks) that attraction and
relational energy are driven by a difference in "charge" — one pole more
structured/directive, the other more responsive/emotive — regardless of
which partner holds which. Worth separating the descriptive claim (this
pattern shows up) from the prescriptive claim (you should cultivate a
particular pole).
