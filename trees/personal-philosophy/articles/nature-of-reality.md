# Nature of Reality

[← Personal Philosophy](../index.md) · `PP_REALITY`

Whether there is a reality independent of observers, and if so whether it
is knowable. Realism holds that things are what they are regardless of
anyone's beliefs; idealism and various constructivisms hold that what we
call reality is partly or wholly constituted by mind, language, or social
agreement. The stakes are high downstream: an objective ethics is much
harder to build on a reality that is itself negotiable.

## Open questions

- Is reality independent of consciousness — and how would I know either
  way without begging the question?
- What is the strongest anti-realist argument, stated so that it doesn't
  sound absurd?
- Does the existence of a mind-independent world guarantee anything about
  our access to it?
- Where do abstractions — numbers, universals, categories — live? Are they
  discovered or constructed?
- Does anything in how I actually live depend on the answer?
