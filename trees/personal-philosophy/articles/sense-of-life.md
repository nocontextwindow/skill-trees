# Sense of Life

[← Personal Philosophy](../index.md) · `PP_SENSE`

The pre-conceptual, emotionally integrated appraisal of existence that
operates beneath explicit belief — roughly, whether the universe reads as
benevolent and worth engaging or as hostile and to be endured. It's
treated in some accounts as an implicit metaphysics: it shapes what feels
possible before any argument is made, which is why it can conflict with
stated philosophy and usually wins when it does.

## Open questions

- What is my sense of life, stated plainly — and does it match the
  philosophy I claim?
- Where did it come from, and how much of it is revisable?
- Can a sense of life be deliberately changed, or only slowly shifted by
  what you expose yourself to?
- Is a benevolent-universe premise a factual claim, a psychological
  stance, or a choice?
- What does my involuntary reaction to other people's success or failure
  reveal about it?
