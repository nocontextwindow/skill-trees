# Art, Beauty & Taste

[← Personal Philosophy](../index.md) · `PP_ART`

What art does that argument can't. One account holds that art
concretizes a sense of life — it doesn't argue for a view of existence, it
lets you inhabit one, which is why it moves people who are unmoved by the
corresponding proposition. That raises the question of whether aesthetic
judgment is objective, subjective, or objective-relative-to-a-standard,
and whether some art is better than other art in any sense stronger than
preference.

## Open questions

- Is aesthetic value objective, and if not, what makes some taste better
  than other taste?
- What is art *for* — projection of the ideal, depiction of the real,
  emotional exercise, something else?
- Why does music move sense of life so directly while bypassing the
  conceptual level almost entirely?
- Which art actually reflects the sense of life I want versus the one I
  have?
- Can deliberately chosen art reshape a sense of life, or only express it?
