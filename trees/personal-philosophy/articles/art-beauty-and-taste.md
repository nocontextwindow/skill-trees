# Art, Beauty & Taste

`PP_ART` · [← Personal Philosophy](../index.md)

What art does that argument can't, and whether aesthetic judgment can be right or wrong.

## Questions

- Is aesthetic value objective? If not, what makes some taste better than other taste?
- What is art for — projection of the ideal, depiction of the real, emotional exercise?
- Why does music move sense of life so directly while bypassing the conceptual level?
- Which art reflects the sense of life I want versus the one I have?
- Can chosen art reshape a sense of life, or only express it?

## Challenges & perspectives

- Art as concretized sense of life: it lets you inhabit a view of existence rather than argue for it.
- Kant: judgments of beauty are subjective but claim universal assent.
- Hume's standard of taste: the verdict of qualified critics over time.
- Institutional theory: art is whatever the artworld treats as art.
- Romantic realism vs. naturalism as a live disagreement about what art should depict.
