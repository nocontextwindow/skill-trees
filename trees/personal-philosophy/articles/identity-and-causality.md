# Identity & Causality

[← Personal Philosophy](../index.md) · `PP_IDENTITY`

That a thing is what it is, and acts according to what it is. This is the
axiom-level machinery: identity underwrites the possibility of knowledge
(a thing with no determinate nature can't be known), and causality is
often treated as identity applied to action rather than as a separate
brute fact about event sequences. The Humean challenge — that we observe
constant conjunction and infer necessity we never actually perceive —
never fully goes away.

## Open questions

- Is causality a feature of reality or a feature of how we model it?
- Does the law of identity do real work, or is it an empty tautology
  dressed up as a foundation?
- What survives of identity when the entity in question changes over time —
  including me?
- Can something be genuinely uncaused, and would that break anything I
  need?
- How much does a macroscopic account of causality need to answer to
  physics?
