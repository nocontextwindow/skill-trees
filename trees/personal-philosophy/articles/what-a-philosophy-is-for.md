# What a Philosophy Is For

[← Personal Philosophy](../index.md) · `PP_WHATFOR`

Before building a system, decide what the system is supposed to do.
Candidate jobs: describe reality accurately, tell you how to act, hold a
group together, make life bearable, or make life *worth* it. These come
apart — a philosophy optimized for consolation is not the same one you'd
build for accuracy, and a philosophy optimized for social cohesion may not
be true at all. The choice of job constrains everything downstream, so it
can't be left implicit.

## Open questions

- What is my philosophy actually *for* — and am I honest about which job I
  want it to do?
- If a philosophy were true but corrosive to live by, would I want it?
  What does that answer reveal?
- Is it possible to *not* run on a philosophy, or only to run on an
  unexamined one?
- How much of what I currently believe was chosen versus absorbed?
- Does a philosophy have to be livable by others, or only by me?
