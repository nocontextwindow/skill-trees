# Meaning & Purpose

[← Personal Philosophy](../index.md) · `PP_MEANING`

Whether meaning is found — discovered by aligning with something already
there, a nature, a telos, a tradition — or made, constructed through
commitment in a universe that supplies none. The existentialist line takes
the second and treats the absence of given purpose as the precondition for
authentic choice. Both answers have to deal with the observation that
constructed meaning often fails to feel like meaning to the person
constructing it.

## Open questions

- Is meaning discovered or constructed — and does a constructed meaning
  actually function like a found one?
- Does meaning require something larger than oneself, or is that a
  smuggled premise?
- What distinguishes a meaningful life from a merely satisfying one?
- Can suffering be meaningful, or only be made bearable by meaning
  imported from elsewhere?
- If nothing is given, does anything follow about what to choose?
