## Hermeticism & The Kybalion (`ES_HERMET`)

*The Kybalion* (1908, attributed to "Three Initiates") popularized seven
"Hermetic principles" (mentalism, correspondence, vibration, polarity,
rhythm, cause and effect, gender) as a condensed New Thought reading of
much older Hermetic philosophy (the *Corpus Hermeticum*, late antiquity).
Worth separating: what's actually in the ancient Hermetic corpus, versus
what's a 1908 repackaging aimed at an early-20th-century self-help
audience.

## Gnosticism — God vs the Devil, Dualism (`ES_GNOSTIC`)

Gnostic cosmology frames the material world as the flawed creation of a
lesser, sometimes malevolent demiurge, with a true, transcendent God beyond
it — salvation is escape via secret knowledge (*gnosis*), not obedience or
faith. Where does this dualistic frame actually help explain moral
experience (the sense that the world as given is somehow broken or
adversarial), and where does it just become an excuse for
world-rejection?
