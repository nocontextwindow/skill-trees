## Secular Spirituality Synthesis (`ES_SECULAR`)

The synthesis node: after surveying comparative religion, mysticism,
Hermeticism/Gnosticism, and occult symbolic frameworks, what actually
carries over into a rational, non-supernatural worldview? Candidate answer
shape: the "spiritual" names a real domain (meaning, orientation, the
felt-sense of value — see Personal Philosophy's `PP_SENSE`) without
requiring supernatural metaphysics to back it.
