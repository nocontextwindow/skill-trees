## Occult Symbolism & Practice (`ES_OCCULT`)

Tarot, astrology, ceremonial magic, alchemy — treated here not as literal
causal claims but as symbolic systems: structured vocabularies for
psychological states, archetypes, and decision-framing (Jung took this
approach seriously, especially with alchemy as a projection of
individuation). What's the actual functional value of these systems as
frameworks for reflection, independent of any supernatural claims?

## Meta-Ethics of Magic Systems (`ES_MAGIC_ETHICS`)

Fictional magic systems (hard vs. soft magic, cost-based systems like
Sanderson's, "the price is the point" systems) are miniature ethical
thought experiments — they force explicit tradeoffs (power for cost, e.g.
life-years, sanity, moral corruption) that real-world ethics usually leaves
implicit. Which fictional systems actually encode a coherent ethical
claim, and which are just power fantasy with rules attached?
