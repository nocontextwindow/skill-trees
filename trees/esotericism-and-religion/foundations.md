## Comparative Religion — Survey (`ES_COMPREL`)

A working survey of the major traditions (Christianity, Judaism, Islam,
Hinduism, Buddhism, and the smaller esoteric/mystery traditions), not for
theological accuracy debates but to see the recurring structural elements:
a cosmology, a moral code, a ritual practice, an eschatology, and a
community-cohesion mechanism. What's actually common across all of them,
and what's specific to each?

## Mysticism vs Rationalism (`ES_MYSTIC`)

Mystical traditions across religions converge on similar claims (ego
dissolution, direct unmediated experience of "the ground of being," the
inadequacy of language to describe it) despite wildly different theological
scaffolding. Is that convergence evidence of something real being pointed
at, or just a predictable output of how human brains behave under certain
conditions (meditation, fasting, ritual, altered states)? Where does
rationalism have to concede something to mysticism, if anywhere?
