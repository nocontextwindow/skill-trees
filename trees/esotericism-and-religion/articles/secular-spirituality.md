# Secular Spirituality Synthesis

[← Esotericism & Religion](../index.md) · `ES_SECULAR`

The project of keeping what religion provides — meaning, awe, community,
discipline, rites of passage, a workable stance toward death — without
accepting the supernatural claims that came attached. The obvious hazard is
cherry-picking: taking the consoling parts while dropping the demanding
ones, when the demands may be exactly what made the consolations work.
Traditions are packages with load-bearing parts, and which parts those are
is not established. There's also an authority problem that rarely gets
stated plainly: religious practice draws much of its force from being
not-optional and not-self-authored, and anything you assemble yourself can
be disassembled by the same authority the moment it becomes inconvenient.

## Open questions

- Which functions of religion are separable from its metaphysics, and which
  turn out to be load-bearing once you remove them?
- Can a self-authored practice generate real obligation, or does obligation
  require a source outside yourself?
- Is a community indispensable to this, or can it genuinely be done alone?
- What would count as evidence that a secular substitute is working, rather
  than just feeling good?
- Is honest participation possible in a tradition whose claims you reject —
  and what do you owe the people there who mean it?
