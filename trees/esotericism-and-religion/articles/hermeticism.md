# Hermeticism

[← Esotericism & Religion](../index.md) · `ES_HERMET`

A late-antique Greco-Egyptian body of writing with a cosmology of
correspondence: mind as the substrate of the cosmos, the microcosm
mirroring the macrocosm, ascent of the soul through the spheres. It matters
historically far out of proportion to its size, feeding Renaissance magic,
alchemy, and most of what later became Western esotericism. There is also a
modern layer routinely mistaken for the ancient one — nineteenth- and
twentieth-century systematizations that state tidy "hermetic principles" in
a far more mentalist, self-help-shaped form than the original texts
support. Separating the historical tradition from its later repackaging is
most of the work here, and the repackaging is what almost everyone
encounters first.

## Open questions

- Which parts of "hermeticism" are late antique and which are modern
  reconstruction — and does the distinction change how seriously to take
  either?
- Is the doctrine of correspondence a metaphysical claim, an analogy engine
  for generating thought, or a poetic style?
- Alchemy: literal transmutation, psychological process, or both at
  different times to different practitioners?
- What did the mentalist "all is mind" reading actually assert before it
  became a motivational slogan?
- Why does this framework keep getting revived? What is it giving people
  that the available alternatives don't?
