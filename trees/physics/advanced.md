## Quantum Field Theory (`PH_QFT`)

Merges quantum mechanics with special relativity by treating particles as
quantized excitations of underlying fields rather than as fixed objects —
which is what makes particle creation and annihilation possible and
resolves the tension between fixed particle number in ordinary QM and
mass-energy equivalence from `PH_SR`. The Standard Model is a QFT built
from gauge symmetries (local invariances that dictate the form of the
force-carrying fields). The path-integral formulation (Feynman) and
perturbation theory via Feynman diagrams are the main computational
toolkit; renormalization — systematically absorbing the infinities that
naive calculations produce — is one of the more conceptually strange but
essential techniques you'll need to actually get numbers out.

## Particle Physics (`PH_PARTICLE`)

The experimental and phenomenological application of QFT: the Standard
Model catalogs the known fermions (quarks, leptons) and force-carrying
bosons (photon, W/Z, gluons, Higgs), organized by the gauge group
SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1). The 2012 discovery of the Higgs boson at the LHC
confirmed the mechanism by which most Standard Model particles acquire
mass. Open problems — neutrino masses (not predicted by the minimal
Standard Model), the matter-antimatter asymmetry of the universe, dark
matter's particle identity (if it has one), and the absence so far of any
supersymmetric partners — define most of the field's current experimental
program.

## Cosmology (`PH_COSMO`)

Applies GR to the universe as a whole via the Friedmann-Lemaître-
Robertson-Walker metric, describing an expanding universe from an initial
hot dense state (the Big Bang) through inflation, nucleosynthesis, and
structure formation. The current concordance model (ΛCDM) fits precision
data (the cosmic microwave background, large-scale structure, supernova
distances) extremely well while requiring dark matter (~27% of the
universe's energy density) and dark energy (~68%) whose fundamental nature
is unknown — arguably the largest open empirical mystery in physics today.

## Condensed Matter Physics (`PH_COND`)

The physics of the many-body systems that make up ordinary matter — how
huge numbers of interacting particles produce emergent collective
phenomena that look nothing like their individual constituents: band
structure and semiconductors, superconductivity (BCS theory, and the still
poorly understood high-temperature variety), magnetism, phase transitions
and critical phenomena. It's the most direct link back to `PH_THERMO` and
`PH_QM` simultaneously, and arguably the subfield of physics with the
biggest footprint in actual technology (transistors, lasers, MRI machines
all come out of condensed matter).
