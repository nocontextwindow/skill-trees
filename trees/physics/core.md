## Electromagnetism (`PH_EM`)

Maxwell's four equations unify electricity, magnetism, and optics into a
single field theory, and turn out (via the Lorentz transformation of E and
B into each other) to be secretly relativistic before relativity was
discovered — a fact Einstein leaned on directly. Working through Maxwell's
equations in integral and differential form, plus the wave equation they
imply for light, is the concrete payoff of the vector calculus grind in
`PH_MATH`. It's also the last major classical theory before physics starts
needing genuinely new conceptual frameworks (relativity, then quantum
mechanics).

## Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics (`PH_THERMO`)

Thermodynamics gives the macroscopic laws (energy conservation, entropy
increase, the impossibility of perfect heat engines) purely
phenomenologically; statistical mechanics explains *why* those laws hold by
counting microstates, showing that entropy is (up to Boltzmann's constant)
the logarithm of the number of microstates consistent with a macrostate.
This is one of the great reductions in physics — thermodynamamic
irreversibility emerging from time-reversible microscopic laws purely as a
matter of overwhelming statistical likelihood — and it's also the direct
mathematical ancestor of energy-based and diffusion models in modern
machine learning (see `ML_GEN`).

**Resources:**

## Waves & Optics (`PH_WAVES`)

Wave phenomena — interference, diffraction, polarization, standing waves —
recur across mechanics, electromagnetism, and eventually quantum mechanics,
which is part of why this sits as a hub node before special relativity.
The wave equation derived from Maxwell's equations predicts light
propagating at a fixed speed c independent of the medium's rest frame,
which is precisely the puzzle (irreconcilable with Galilean relativity)
that special relativity was built to resolve.
