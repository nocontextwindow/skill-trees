## Philosophy of Physics: QM Interpretations / Space & Time (`PH_PHILO`)

Quantum mechanics gives correct predictions without settling what's
actually happening between measurements — the interpretations (Copenhagen,
many-worlds/Everettian, pilot-wave/de Broglie-Bohm, objective collapse
models like GRW, QBism) agree on every experimental prediction so far and
differ only on ontology, which is part of why the debate has run for a
century without empirical resolution. On the relativity side, the
philosophy of space and time asks whether spacetime is a substance in its
own right (substantivalism) or just a system of relations among events
(relationalism), and whether the "block universe" picture implied by
special relativity — where past, present, and future are all equally real —
should be taken at face value or is compatible with a meaningful sense of
temporal becoming. This node is where `PH_QM` and `PH_GR` both feed
directly into general philosophy of science (`EP_SCI`).
