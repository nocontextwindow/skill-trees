## Conversational Skill / Charisma (`SOC_CONVO`)

Charisma decomposes into legible sub-skills: presence (being fully in the
interaction, not distracted), warmth (signaling positive intent), and power
(signaling capability) — most "naturally charismatic" people are just
strong on all three simultaneously.

## Networking (`SOC_NETWORK`)

Building a durable web of relationships before you need anything from it —
the actual skill is being useful/interesting to people over a long time
horizon, not transactional asks.
