## Communication Fundamentals (`SOC_COMM`)

Clarity, active listening, and the difference between talking *at* someone
and actually exchanging information/intent with them. Most communication
failures are listening failures, not speaking failures.

## Storytelling & Narrative Framing (`SOC_STORY`)

Structuring information as a story (setup, tension, resolution) rather
than a list of facts — dramatically more memorable and persuasive, because
it lets the listener experience the point instead of just being told it.
